# Privacy Policy for Lumo

**Effective date: October 10, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Lumo handles information when you use the Lumo notes app on Apple platforms. It covers the app, its optional iCloud sync, image features, location features, and in-app purchase flow.

## Information Lumo handles

### Notes and images

Notes you create are stored on your device. A note may contain text, formatting, and images you choose to add. On iPhone and iPad, Lumo's optional photo text recognition feature uses Apple Vision on the device to recognize text in a photo you select. Lumo does not send your notes or selected images to a server operated by the Lumo developer for advertising or AI training.

### iCloud sync

When iCloud is available and enabled for the app, Lumo uses Apple's CloudKit service to sync your notes and their attachments between devices using your iCloud account. Note data is stored in the app's private iCloud database. The Lumo developer does not operate a separate account or sync server for this content and does not have a dashboard for reading your private notes. Apple processes and stores data as needed to provide iCloud and CloudKit, subject to Apple's terms and privacy information.

If iCloud is unavailable or you are signed out, you can continue to use the app with information stored on your device. Changes made offline may sync when iCloud becomes available again.

### Optional location

Location access is optional. Lumo requests access only when you choose to add a location to a note, or when you create a note while automatic location is enabled. Automatic location is off by default. Lumo requests a one-time, in-use location; it does not track your location in the background. The location and the place name returned for it are saved with the note and may sync to your private iCloud database.

To find a place name for coordinates, Lumo uses Apple's Core Location or MapKit geocoding service. Apple may process the coordinates to return a place name under Apple's applicable terms and privacy information. You can deny or revoke location access in your device settings. Without access, you can continue using other note features and can remove a saved location from a note.

### Photos and image features

When you add a photo or choose a profile image, Lumo uses Apple's system photo picker on iPhone and iPad, or a system file picker on Mac, and receives only the item you select. It does not request permission to browse your entire photo library. A photo you add to a note is saved as note content and, if iCloud sync is enabled, may sync with that note.

If you choose **Save Image** for a note's share image, Lumo requests permission to add that image to your Photos library. This permission is separate from selecting a photo. You can decline it and continue to use Lumo; you can still share the image using Apple's share sheet.

### Profile information, settings, and purchases

If you set a nickname or profile image, Lumo stores that profile on your device and syncs it through your private iCloud database when sync is available. You can change or reset your profile in the app.

Lumo stores app preferences, recent searches, and review history on your device to remember choices and support app features. Lumo does not require a Lumo account, and does not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

If you make an in-app purchase, Apple processes the transaction through the App Store. Lumo does not receive your Apple Account password or payment card details. The app stores purchase information needed to verify and restore your access.

## How information is used and shared

Lumo uses information to save, display, search, edit, and sync your notes; apply requested location and image features; remember app settings; and verify purchases. The app does not sell your personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or use your note content to train AI models.

Information is sent to Apple when needed for iCloud and CloudKit sync, location lookup, App Store transactions, or Apple system features you choose to use. When you explicitly use the share sheet or export a file, information is sent to the destination you select. Those Apple services and destinations handle information under their own terms and privacy policies.

## Storage, retention, and deletion

Notes remain on your device and, when sync is enabled, in your private iCloud database until you delete them. Deleted notes move to the app's Recently Deleted area, where they can be restored for 30 days. You can permanently delete a note sooner. The app purges expired items during its cleanup process. Permanent deletion also removes the note's attached images and note history from the app, and the deletion syncs to your other devices when iCloud is available.

Profile information remains until you change or reset it. App preferences remain on your device while stored by the app. Exported files are separate files; manage or delete them in Files or at the location where you saved or shared them.

Deleting Lumo from one device does not necessarily delete copies already synced to iCloud or downloaded to your other devices. Manage synced data from Lumo and your Apple account and devices. iCloud synchronization and deletion can take time, particularly while a device is offline. Apple handles its own service-side retention and backups.

## Your choices

You can:

- Deny or revoke location access in device settings, and turn automatic location off in Lumo.
- Choose which photos to share with Lumo using Apple's system picker.
- Decline permission to save share images to Photos.
- Export, share, restore, or permanently delete notes in Lumo; reset your nickname or profile image in the app.
- Manage iCloud and App Store purchases through your Apple account and device settings.

To ask a question about this policy or Lumo's privacy practices, contact us at [conciseclock@gmail.com](mailto:conciseclock@gmail.com). Do not email sensitive note content.

## Privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Lumo does not keep a separate developer-operated account or server containing your notes. You can manage note content in Lumo, and manage iCloud data using your Apple account and devices. If you need help exercising a privacy right, contact us at [conciseclock@gmail.com](mailto:conciseclock@gmail.com). We will handle requests as required by applicable law.

## Children

Lumo is a general-purpose notes app and is not designed specifically for children. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of iCloud, the photo library, and other device services.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Policy when Lumo's features or data practices change. The effective date above shows when this version took effect. If a change materially affects how information is handled, we will provide any notice required by applicable law.
